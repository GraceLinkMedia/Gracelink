    answer: "A welcoming environment for all nations and generations. Heartfelt praise & worship – lively with simple, singable words. Christ-centred, dynamic Bible teaching that's relevant to your daily life."

    answer: "Your 'Sunday best' is simply you! Jeans, trainers, T-shirts, or smart-casual—whatever makes you comfortable is perfect. We like to look good, but we're not overly formal. Just come as you are, and you'll fit right in."
