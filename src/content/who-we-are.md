whatWeBelieve:
  heading: What We Believe
  image: assets/images/who-we-are-believe.png
  imageAlt: An open Bible resting on a wooden table
  beliefs:
    - "There is only one Eternal God and is the Trinity of the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit. He is the creator and sustainer of all things."
    - "Jesus Christ is the only Son of God. He came from heaven, was born of Virgin Mary through the operation of the Holy Spirit. As man and God at the same time, he lived on earth, was crucified, died, was buried, rose from the dead and ascended to heaven where He is seated at the right hand of the Father. Jesus Christ is the only way by which we can get to the Father."
    - "The Holy Spirit, true God proceeding from the Father and the Son, convicts the world of sin, righteousness and judgement and leads in all truth."
    - "The Bible is God's word, a proclamation of the perfect will of God the Father, written by the inspiration of the Holy Spirit and is infallible."
    - "Man is created in the image of God; due to their sinful rebellion, this image is marred. All man need to be saved by receiving personal salvation through the redemptive work of Jesus Christ."
    - "GraceLink Church believes in water baptism by immersion and the Lord's Supper as ordinances to be observed by the Church in this present age."
    - "GraceLink Church believes in baptism in the Holy Spirit and Power with the initial evidence of speaking in tongues as promised to all believers. She believes in the fruit of the Spirit in the life of a Christian and the manifestation of the gifts of the Spirit for service."
    - "Jesus Christ is the Head of the Church, which is constituted by the Holy Spirit and consists of born again believers. As a community of believers, they fellowship with and edify one another. The Church is responsible for the proclamation and demonstration of the gospel with healings, miracles, signs and wonders following."
    - "GraceLink Church believes that at the time appointed by God the Father, Jesus Christ, the Son of God will come to take away his bride, the Church."
    - "GraceLink Church believes in the resurrection of the dead, the eternal bliss of the saved and the eternal torment of the lost. She believes in the new heaven and the new earth where God will reign in glory."
